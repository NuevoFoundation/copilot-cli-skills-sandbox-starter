CHALLENGE 02: Give volunteer knowledge a small, readable blog
FROM: Mollee Jain, COO, Nuevo Foundation
CONTEXT: Workshop request featuring a real NF teammate, not a quoted message.
PRIORITY: Optional. One feature and one delivery record at a time.

GO-TO
Mollee thinks we could use a small blog to share useful volunteer knowledge.
The production NF site has a blog. Build a small, offline version for this
practice site so a volunteer can scan three useful articles and read one in
full. This is a static-content feature, not a publishing platform.

GROUNDING
Read challenge-data/content.json: about.draftNotes and blogDrafts.
All three supplied articles are original workshop samples. Preserve their
sample labels. Do not claim they are existing published NF stories, add fake
publication dates, or attribute them to a real team member.

SCOPE
Create blog.html and three local article pages named after the supplied slugs.
Use styles.css for shared styling. Add one Blog link to the homepage's
"Workshop pages" footer navigation. Every article needs a return link to the
blog and home. Keep the complete supplied paragraphs, not summary-only pages.

ACCEPTANCE
1. The blog shows exactly three title/category/summary cards and working links.
2. Each local article page shows its full supplied text and sample attribution.
3. No WordPress, CMS, comments, accounts, analytics, remote fonts, or live feed.
4. Headings are hierarchical; links have meaningful names and visible focus.
5. The index and all three articles work offline at 390px and 1440px.
